# Manual updates

## 25 September 2026 · Clickie 4.2.4

- **Asset matrix:** compare sites or equipment by period, group metrics by tags and configure calculations and totals. [Full guide with a real widget](analisis/matriz-activos.md).
- **Shared resources:** grant and revoke direct access to assets, metrics and dashboards for limited collaborators, and review inherited access. [Sharing guide](configuracion/recursos-compartidos.md).
- **Collaborators and profiles:** guided enrollment with email or private delivery, access management, registration information when available and activity identifying the affected resource. [Account settings](configuracion/cuenta.md).
- **Selectors:** easier asset customization, tag suggestions and simplified single-metric selection.
- **Navigation:** menus remain available while home and dashboard widgets load their data.
- **Downloads:** improved PNG exports and CSV downloads for snapshots, comparisons and asset matrices, including matrix rows outside the visible area.
- **Schedules:** more consistent handling of special days and extensions across multiple groups.
- **Thermostats:** clearer missing or outdated readings and retries; manual controls follow Clickie Smart control status.

Earlier screenshots remain valid for unchanged features. The new matrix example uses illustrative assets and existing measurements.
