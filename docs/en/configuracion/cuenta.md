# Account settings

Account settings bring together the organization's details and shared preferences. Check which account is active before changing these values.

## Account details

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Identify the account in lists, selectors and navigation. |
| Description | Store an internal reference. |
| Picture | Identify the account visually. |
| Country and tax ID | Define its tax and regional context. |
| Main time zone | Set the operating time zone for indicators, monitors and account processes. |
| Optimized time zones | Specify other time zones where data delivery is optimized. |
| Responsible collaborator | Assign the collaborator used for account processes. |
| Home dashboard | Choose the dashboard that opens on the home page. |
| Optional fields | Complete additional attributes used by the account. |

Edit the necessary fields and save the form. Required fields are marked on screen.

## Example: always open the plant dashboard

1. Open the settings for **Planta de demostración**.
2. In **Home dashboard**, select the plant's monitoring dashboard.
3. Save and return to **Home** to open it.

## Collaborators

In **Configuration > Account Management > Collaborators**, view people with access and, if permitted, change their role, language and tags. Blocking someone prevents access without deleting them.

Each collaborator's permissions determine which modules and actions are available.

## Appearance

In **Configuration > Account Management > Appearance**, configure the site name, sign-in text, logos, sign-in image, browser tab icon and brand color. The subdomain defines the account's access address.

- **Authentication logo**: appears on the sign-in page.
- **Internal logo**: appears in the header and menu.
- **Tab icon**: identifies the browser tab.

The screen indicates when the account inherits its environment's appearance. Removing its own appearance restores the environment settings.

See also [Data and sources](./datos-y-fuentes.md).

## Add a collaborator

From **Collaborators**, start enrollment and follow the steps to identify the person and assign their account role and access level. Available options depend on your permissions. Existing users retain their identity and password; you do not need to create another identity to associate them with an account.

When registering a new user, choose **send by email** to send the welcome message with access instructions, or **share privately** to deliver the temporary password yourself. The latter does not send a welcome email. Deliver the password privately to the intended person.

The **role** determines available actions. The **access level** determines which resources they can find in the platform. For limited collaborators, see [Shared resources](recursos-compartidos.md).

## Collaborator profile and activity

The profile brings together overview, activity and shared resources. Check the last sign-in and, where recorded, the registration date and who registered or associated the person with the account. Missing historical information does not mean the registration is recent.

Activity describes the action, affected resource and available change details; for example, an asset rename with its previous and new names. Use it to understand account changes. Visible information depends on your permissions.

## Your profile and security

From the user menu, review your personal details, photo, language, time zone and communication preferences. Email, password and multifactor authentication options include their verification steps. You can review access history and, if permitted, copy your API key; it remains masked on screen until needed.

After switching accounts, check the active account before editing a collaborator or sharing a resource: available options follow your permissions in that account.
