# Shared resources

Share assets, metrics and dashboards with account collaborators who have limited access. These people can see resources they create or are assigned. People with access to all account resources do not need individual sharing.

## Grant or revoke access

1. Open the resource's action menu and choose **Share**, when your role permits it.
2. Search for the collaborator. The dialog lists people with limited access; people not listed already have access through their access level.
3. Check their access status and use the option to grant or revoke direct access.
4. Close the dialog when finished.

If there are no limited-access collaborators, the dialog explains that everyone has access to the resources. Individual assignments are unnecessary.

## What sharing includes

| Resource | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Asset | Includes descendant assets and associated metrics. |
| Metric | Makes that metric discoverable among the platform's available resources. |
| Dashboard | Includes subdashboards and allows viewing widget data. It does not, by itself, grant access to the entire metric catalog or editing rights. |

The person's role still determines which actions they can perform. Sharing does not make them an administrator. Review each account separately: membership in one account does not grant access to others.

## Direct, inherited and own-resource access

**Direct** access is assigned to the resource itself. **Inherited** access comes from another shared resource, such as a parent asset or dashboard. People can also access resources they created.

Revoking a direct assignment does not remove other access routes. If the resource remains visible, check whether it belongs to a shared asset or dashboard, was created by that person, or is available through their access level.

## Review assignments

In **Shared with me**, view resources available through assignments. In a collaborator's profile, the **Shared resources** tab lets you review and manage assignments according to your permissions. Use filters and search to find a resource. Direct access appears before inherited access; only resources you can view are listed.
